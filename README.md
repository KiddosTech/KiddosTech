## 🌱 About Me

Hi, I’m **KiddosTech** — a young Indonesian developer who enjoys turning ideas into real projects. I love learning through building: making tools, experimenting with new technologies, and creating software that is useful, fun, and practical.

I’m especially interested in:

- Web development with Vue, TypeScript, and JavaScript

I believe that curiosity, consistency, and creativity matter more than age. Every project I build is a step toward becoming a better developer.
